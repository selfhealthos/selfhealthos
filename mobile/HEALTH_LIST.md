Here's the list cleaned up and organized into a numbered checklist:

### Sleep & Recovery

1. Sleep is the world's most powerful drug.
2. Be in bed for 8 hours.
3. Go to bed at the same time every night.
4. Sleep before midnight when possible.
5. Don't eat right before bed.
6. Eat calming foods for dinner.
7. No screens 1 hour before bed.
8. Read a book before bed.
9. Have a 1-hour wind-down routine.
10. Sleep in a cold room.
11. Avoid bright lights after sunset.
12. The body is a clock and loves routine.

### Nutrition

13. Eat whole foods.
14. Avoid added sugar.
15. Avoid fried foods.
16. Avoid most foods sold in convenience stores.
17. Drink enough water.
18. Finish coffee before noon.
19. Alcohol is bad for you.

### Exercise & Movement

20. Walk a little after meals.
21. Or do air squats after meals.
22. Get your heart rate high regularly.
23. Lift heavy things (strength training).
24. Stretch daily.
25. Avoid sitting for long periods.
26. Stand up straight.
